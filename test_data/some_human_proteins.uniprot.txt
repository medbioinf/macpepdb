CC   -!- FUNCTION: Subunit of both mTORC1 and mTORC2, which regulates cell
CC       growth and survival in response to nutrient and hormonal signals.
CC       mTORC1 is activated in response to growth factors or amino acids. In
CC       response to nutrients, mTORC1 is recruited to the lysosome membrane and
CC       promotes protein, lipid and nucleotide synthesis by phosphorylating
CC       several substrates, such as ribosomal protein S6 kinase (RPS6KB1 and
CC       RPS6KB2) and EIF4EBP1 (4E-BP1). In the same time, it inhibits catabolic
CC       pathways by phosphorylating the autophagy initiation components ULK1
CC       and ATG13, as well as transcription factor TFEB, a master regulators of
CC       lysosomal biogenesis and autophagy. The mTORC1 complex is inhibited in
CC       response to starvation and amino acid depletion. Within mTORC1, MLST8
CC       interacts directly with MTOR and enhances its kinase activity. In
CC       nutrient-poor conditions, stabilizes the MTOR-RPTOR interaction and
CC       favors RPTOR-mediated inhibition of MTOR activity. As part of the
CC       mTORC2 complex, transduces signals from growth factors to pathways
CC       involved in proliferation, cytoskeletal organization, lipogenesis and
CC       anabolic output. mTORC2 is also activated by growth factors, but seems
CC       to be nutrient-insensitive. In response to growth factors, mTORC2
CC       phosphorylates and activates AGC protein kinase family members,
CC       including AKT (AKT1, AKT2 and AKT3), PKC (PRKCA, PRKCB and PRKCE) and
CC       SGK1. mTORC2 functions upstream of Rho GTPases to regulate the actin
CC       cytoskeleton, probably by activating one or more Rho-type guanine
CC       nucleotide exchange factors. mTORC2 promotes the serum-induced
CC       formation of stress-fibers or F-actin. mTORC2 plays a critical role in
CC       AKT1 activation by mediating phosphorylation of different sites
CC       depending on the context, such as 'Thr-450', 'Ser-473', 'Ser-477' or
CC       'Thr-479', facilitating the phosphorylation of the activation loop of
CC       AKT1 on 'Thr-308' by PDPK1/PDK1 which is a prerequisite for full
CC       activation. mTORC2 regulates the phosphorylation of SGK1 at 'Ser-422'.
CC       mTORC2 also modulates the phosphorylation of PRKCA on 'Ser-657'. Within
CC       mTORC2, MLST8 acts as a bridge between MAPKAP1/SIN1 and MTOR.
CC       {ECO:0000256|RuleBase:RU369068}.
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Part of the mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1 (mTORC1)
CC       which contains MTOR, MLST8 and RPTOR. mTORC1 associates with
CC       AKT1S1/PRAS40, which inhibits its activity. mTORC1 binds to and is
CC       inhibited by FKBP12-rapamycin. Within mTORC1, interacts directly with
CC       MTOR and RPTOR. Component of the mechanistic target of rapamycin
CC       complex 2 (mTORC2), consisting in two heterotretramers composed of
CC       MTOR, MLST8, RICTOR and MAPKAP1/SIN1. Contrary to mTORC1, mTORC2 does
CC       not bind to and is not sensitive to FKBP12-rapamycin. mTORC1 and mTORC2
CC       associate with DEPTOR, which regulates their activity. Interacts with
CC       RHEB. Interacts with MEAK7. Interacts with SIK3. Interacts with
CC       SLC38A7; this interaction promotes the recruitment of mTORC1 to the
CC       lysosome and its subsequent activation. {ECO:0000256|ARBA:ARBA00066056,
CC       ECO:0000256|RuleBase:RU369068}.
